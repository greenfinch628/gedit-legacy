# Changelog

## 50.0-3 — 2026-10-08

- Add Open dropdown → Workspace with a directory-only selector; Select replaces the file-browser tree and Cancel preserves it.
- Double-click folders to make their contents the sidebar’s current view.

- Add an accessible × button at the top right of the sidebar to hide it.

## 50.0-2 — 2026-10-08

- Default to the directory tree, with a visible sidebar selector.
- Double-click folders to expand or collapse in place; files open for editing.
- Keep F9 and the View menu for sidebar visibility.
- Rebuild Fedora 44 RPM and Ubuntu 26.04 DEB packages.

## 50.0-1 — based on gedit 50.0

- Preserve and restore untitled documents across orderly application sessions.
- Retain named-file confirmation and individual-tab discard behavior.
- Add a preference for preserving untitled documents on close.
- Show a single-document tab bar and simplify the header.
- Add custom blue-plus artwork and the gedit+ launcher name.
- Prepare local source, licensing, attribution, and distribution documentation.
- Make the sidebar selector visible and expand/collapse browser folders in place on double-click; retain F9 for sidebar visibility.
